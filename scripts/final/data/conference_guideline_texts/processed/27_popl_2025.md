1. **Availability**: Artifact submission is optional but encouraged; must be provided via POPL Artifact Evaluation HotCRP by the set deadlines.
2. **Functionality**: Artifact must run successfully, be consistent with paper claims, and include all components needed for evaluation.
3. **Completeness**: Supports all evaluated claims in the paper; partial artifacts may be accepted if justified.
4. **Documentation**: Must provide clear setup, usage, and reproduction instructions so other researchers can easily replicate results.
5. **Reusability**: Structured to facilitate further research and extension beyond the original study.
6. **Evaluation Process**: Goal is collaborative improvement; most artifacts receive at least one badge; feedback provided early to fix issues before final decision.
7. **Scope**: Accepts software, mechanized proofs, test suites, benchmarks; paper proofs excluded from review due to time and expertise constraints.
8. **Packaging Guidance**: Authors are encouraged to follow detailed packaging and documentation recommendations to ease evaluation.
